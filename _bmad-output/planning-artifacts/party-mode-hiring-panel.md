# Party Mode: Hiring Panel on the Homepage Hero

- **Product:** Sikandar Ali portfolio, homepage hero
- **Version:** 1.0 · **Status:** Final · **Date:** 2026-10-02
- **Workflow:** PM, Party mode (panel played as hiring stakeholders, moderated by Mary)
- **Upstream inputs:** `hero-brainstorm.md`, `ux-spec-hero.md` v1.3, current build (headline, paragraph, CTAs, career timeline, facts row)

## Panel
- VP of Design, health-tech payer (hiring a Lead UX)
- Head of Product, benefits-admin SaaS
- Director of Design Engineering, AI tooling company
- Senior Technical Recruiter

## Findings
1. **The hero says the career three times.** Paragraph ("Before that, Dubai Municipality and Jet Airways"), timeline (four companies with years), facts row (Now / Domain / Led). The timeline is the third telling.
2. **A timeline answers the least interesting question** ("where have you been?"), which the résumé and LinkedIn already answer. VPs hire on judgement and outcomes; the case studies ("Five decisions, told the way they were made") carry that.
3. **The strongest sentence is buried:** "A design system agents can read" is one of four equal stops.
4. **Recruiter dissent:** the timeline is scannable (years, companies) and useful for screening, but the résumé link in the nav covers it.
5. **Session pattern:** many rounds were spent filling a visual slot. The panel reads that as a sign the hero doesn't need a visual; the work is the visual.

## Recommendation (consensus, recruiter partially dissenting)
- Hero becomes **type-only**: status chip, headline, paragraph, two actions, facts row.
- **Move the timeline out of the hero** to where career history belongs (How I work / an About block), not delete it.
- **Pull the work up**: the featured case starts right after the hero so the first scroll lands on real decisions and screens.
- Optional: sharpen the paragraph so it names the AI work, since the timeline no longer carries it.
